Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2025  ·  Pbliz! B.V., Uithoorn, The Netherlands

The short version: our apps do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device. We have no access to it.

Who we are

This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications published by Pbliz! B.V. (also trading as Red Limes Dev), a private limited company registered in the Netherlands, KvK number 50254758, with its registered office at Molenlaan 11E, Uithoorn, The Netherlands.

You can contact us at pbliz@proton.me for any privacy-related questions.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. Our applications are designed to function entirely on your device without transmitting any information to us or to third parties.

Specifically, our apps do not collect: names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, device identifiers, usage data, analytics, crash reports, or any other information that could identify you.

Data storage

Any data generated or entered by you while using our apps is stored locally on your device only. We have no servers, no databases, and no backend systems that receive or store your data.

Third-party services

Our apps do not integrate third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or any other services that could collect data about you.

Children's privacy

Because we do not collect any data, our apps are safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Your rights under GDPR

As a company based in the Netherlands, we operate under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we collect no personal data, there is no data to access, correct, export, or delete. However, if you have any questions about your rights or our practices, please contact us at pbliz@proton.me.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change in the future — for example if a new app collects data — we will update this policy and revise the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at pbliz@proton.me.
